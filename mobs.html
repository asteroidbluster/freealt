Mobs and Creatures

Small behaviors, big surprises.

A fox holding a totem will use it.
Give a fox a totem of undying and it will survive a fatal hit, just like a player would.
Lightning transforms mobs.
A pig struck by lightning becomes a zombified piglin, and a villager struck by lightning becomes a witch.
Zombie sieges ignore light.
At midnight, a horde of zombies can spawn in a village even if every inch is torch-lit — light level doesn't stop them.
You can shear a snow golem's pumpkin off.
Underneath it actually has a face.
Iron golems hand poppies to baby villagers.
Just a little animation, no purpose, but it's there.
Name tag tricks.
Name a vindicator "Johnny" and it attacks everything except illagers, name a rabbit "Toast" for a black-and-white skin, "Dinnerbone" flips any mob upside down, and "jeb_" turns a sheep rainbow.
Evokers do the "wololo."
Their spell turns blue sheep red — a direct Age of Empires priest reference.
Ender pearls have a 5% chance to spawn an endermite — and endermen actively hunt endermites down.
Wool blocks vibrations.
Sculk sensors can't hear anything through wool, snowballs and eggs distract the warden by making noise elsewhere, and the warden's sonic boom ignores armor, shields and walls entirely.
Froglights come from frogs eating small magma cubes,
and the color depends on the frog: temperate frogs make ochre, warm ones pearlescent, cold ones verdant.
Allays duplicate.
Give a dancing allay (near a jukebox) an amethyst shard and it splits into two.

The creaking can't be hurt by hitting it.
You have to find its creaking heart block hidden inside a pale oak tree and break that.
JAVA 1.21.6+ · BEDROCK 1.21.90+
Happy ghasts.
Come from rehydrating a dried ghast in water, and with a harness up to 4 players can ride one.
Java and Bedrock unless noted. The piston door is Java-only (quasi-connectivity doesn't exist on Bedrock). Creaking needs Java 1.21.4+ / Bedrock 1.21.50+; happy ghasts need Java 1.21.6+ / Bedrock 1.21.90+.
