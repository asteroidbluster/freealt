Items, Food and Fun

Suspicious stew's effect depends on the flower fed to the brown mooshroom, and a honey bottle cures poison — the only drink that does.
Crossbows can shoot fireworks.
Load a rocket instead of an arrow and it works, multishot included.
The mace turns falling into damage.
The farther you fall before hitting, the harder it hits — and a successful hit cancels your fall damage completely.
Very rarely, the title screen logo misspells itself as "Minceraft."
Java and Bedrock unless noted. The piston door is Java-only (quasi-connectivity doesn't exist on Bedrock). Creaking needs Java 1.21.4+ / Bedrock 1.21.50+; happy ghasts need Java 1.21.6+ / Bedrock 1.21.90+.
